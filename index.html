HOW TO PLAY
1
🏁
Race to the Chopper
A helicopter waits at the far right of each stage. Sprint, jump, and claw your way to it before your opponent to win the round.
2
🔫
Shoot to Stun
Pick up weapons scattered across the level. Shooting your opponent knocks them back and briefly stuns them — use it to take the lead.
3
⚡
Grab Power-Ups
Glowing pickups drop throughout each round. Speed boosts, shields, and weapons give you a crucial edge in the dash to escape.
4
🏆
Best of 5
First player to win 3 rounds takes the match. Scores are tracked across rounds. Check the leaderboard to see all-time winners.
5
💀
Fall = Lose Round
Fall into the void below the platforms and you lose that round instantly. Platform gaps are deadly — mind your jumps!
6
⏱️
Beat the Clock
Each round has a time limit. If neither player reaches the chopper in time, the round goes to whoever is furthest right on the map.
POWER-UPS
⚡
SPEED BOOST
Doubles your movement speed for 5 seconds. Perfect for dashing ahead when the path is clear.
🛡️
SHIELD
Blocks the next bullet hit. Visual glow shows when shield is active. Essential in tight corridors.
🔫
GUN
Grants one shot. Fire it directly at your opponent to knock them back and steal precious ground.
🦅
TRIPLE JUMP
Temporarily allows a third mid-air jump. Reach higher platforms and shortcuts your opponent can't.
💣
GRENADE
Lobs a bouncing explosive. Wide blast radius can stun your opponent even around corners.
